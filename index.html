WELCOME

Can you tell when an image is misleading?

In this activity, you’ll answer four questions, play a short misinformation-detection game, review a learning guide, and answer four new questions.

Research note: This game uses a fictional celebrity and a clearly labeled simulated social-media post. It does not depict a real person or claim that a real event happened.
  • There are no personal details requested.
  • Your answers are scored for learning, not for judgment.
  • At the end, you can download two CSV files containing your results.
